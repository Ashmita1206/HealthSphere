# HEALTHSPHERE ART DIRECTION SPECIFICATION
**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Concept:** Calm + Alive (Clinical Precision meets Human Editorial Warmth)  
**Canonical Palette:** Warm Off-White (`#FAF9F6`), Deep Clinical Teal (`#0F766E`), Soft Mint (`#E6F4F1`), Slate Ink (`#0F172A`).  

---

## 1. TYPOGRAPHY PERSONALITY
* **Primary UI Font**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` — Geometric precision with human terminals. Communicates clarity, stability, and modern healthcare technology.
* **Editorial Accent Font**: `Instrument Serif` — Applied selectively to patient narrative greetings (e.g., *"Good morning, Alex"*). Infuses emotional warmth and editorial craft without cluttering UI controls.
* **Numeric Data Font**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` with enabled tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums lining-nums`). Guarantees mathematical alignment across vitals and lab reports.

## 2. SHAPE & SURFACE LANGUAGE
* **Borders & Rules**: Thin 1px subtle rules (`#E5E7EB`) take precedence over heavy card containers.
* **Radius Scale**: Controlled radii (`6px` tags, `10px` buttons/inputs, `16px` cards/modals, `9999px` status pills). Over-rounded cards are banned.
* **Background Foundation**: Warm off-white (`#FAF9F6`) in light mode; Slate ink (`#0C1117`) in dark mode.
* **Surfaces**: Primary cards use crisp `#FFFFFF` with `shadow-sm` (`0 1px 2px rgba(15,23,42,0.04)`). Drop-shadow glows and glassmorphism overlays are strictly banned.

## 3. DATA VISUALIZATION LANGUAGE
* **Color Restraint**: Telemetry lines use Deep Teal (`#0F766E`) and Clinical Emerald (`#059669`).
* **Reference Ranges**: Soft tint (`#F0FDFA`) with dashed Teal boundaries (`#14B8A6`).
* **Outliers**: Out-of-range lab parameters use Crimson (`#DC2626`) outlier dots. Rainbow charts and 3D bars are forbidden.

## 4. CLINICAL AI VISUAL LANGUAGE
* **Identity**: Soft Mint (`#E6F4F1`) and Emerald (`#059669`).
* **Attributes**: Transparent evidence attribution (`Verified Lab Baseline`) replacing arbitrary confidence scores.
* **Streaming**: Subtle vertical Emerald pulse bar. Sparkles, robot icons, and glowing purple orbs are prohibited.

## 5. EDITORIAL COMPOSITION & CARD REDUCTION
* **Hierarchy**: Typography $\rightarrow$ Spacing $\rightarrow$ Dividers $\rightarrow$ Surfaces $\rightarrow$ Shadows.
* **Open Layout**: Sections remain un-carded unless content represents a self-contained domain entity (e.g. a lab report record, a medication item).
