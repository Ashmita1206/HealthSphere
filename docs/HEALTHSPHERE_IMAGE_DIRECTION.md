# HEALTHSPHERE IMAGE & VISUAL ART DIRECTION
**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Core Direction:** Authentic, Human, Empathetic, Clinical Restraint  
**Prohibition:** NO generic stock photos of doctors with stethoscope, NO decorative glass blobs, NO artificial 3D floating avatars.  

---

## 1. PHOTOGRAPHY STYLE & PATIENT REPRESENTATION
* **Lighting**: Natural, warm daylight; soft indirect light. Avoid harsh studio flashes or sterile hospital neon.
* **Subjects**: Real, relatable patients in everyday living contexts (working at home, sipping morning tea, taking a walk, reviewing a health report on a tablet).
* **Demographics**: Diverse, multi-generational representation with natural expressions (calm, attentive, empowered).
* **Color Grading**: Warm neutral tones, rich natural slates, teal and emerald accent highlights. Never apply saturated blue or purple color filters.

## 2. MARKETING VS. AUTHENTICATED PRODUCT USAGE
* **Marketing Landing Page (`/`)**:
  * Large, editorial hero images of real patient care moments.
  * Product UI previews framed cleanly in crisp device viewports (`shadow-lg`, thin `1px` slate border).
  * High-contrast visual storytelling paired with `Instrument Serif` headings.
* **Authenticated Product (`/dashboard`, `/reports`, `/ai-chat`, etc.)**:
  * **Zero decorative background imagery**.
  * Icons are functional, structural, and restrained (`lucide-react`, `18px`, `1.75px` stroke).
  * Data visualizations, typography, and crisp open spacing replace imagery.

## 3. EMPTY STATE IMAGE POLICY
* **Rule**: Decorative abstract illustrations and cartoons are strictly prohibited.
* **Standard**: Empty states use a simple, functional Lucide icon inside a soft neutral ring (`#F3F4F1`) paired with explicit 3-part micro-copy: *Context Title $\rightarrow$ Explanatory Body $\rightarrow$ Primary Action Button*.
